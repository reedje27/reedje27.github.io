Terms of Service

Pixel Universe Poster is a private tool. It runs on the owner's own computer and posts the owner's own pixel-art science videos to the owner's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts.

  • Only the owner uses this tool. It is not offered to other people.
  • It posts only videos the owner made and chose to publish.
  • Posts follow the rules of each platform (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok).
  • The tool is provided as is, without any warranty.

Questions: contact the owner through the Pixel Universe YouTube channel.

Privacy Policy

The tool collects no personal data from anyone.

  • Sign-in tokens for the owner's accounts are stored only on the owner's computer and are used only to upload the owner's videos.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • The only data sent to a platform is the video, its caption and its settings, sent to that same platform.
  • The owner can remove access at any time by signing out in the tool or in the platform's settings.
