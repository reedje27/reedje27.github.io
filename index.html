reedje27

reedje27 is a small desktop app made by Raihan Wali. It uploads the short pixel-art science videos of the Pixel Universe channel to the owner's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts.

How it works

  • The owner logs in with TikTok (Login Kit) on his own computer.
  • The owner picks a finished video, writes the caption and chooses who can see it.
  • reedje27 sends the video to TikTok with the Content Posting API.
