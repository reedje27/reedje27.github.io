reedje27 Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

1. Data we use

When you log in with TikTok, reedje27 gets:

  • Your basic TikTok profile info: open ID, display name and profile picture (scope user.info.basic), so the app can show which account is logged in.
  • An access token, so the app can upload videos to your account (scopes video.upload and video.publish).

reedje27 also uses the video file, caption and privacy setting you choose for each post.

2. How we use it

  • Only to show your account in the app and to upload the videos you pick.
  • Never for ads, tracking or profiling.

3. Where it is stored

Tokens and settings are stored only on your own computer. reedje27 has no server and no database. Videos and captions are sent straight to TikTok (or YouTube or Instagram) and nowhere else.

4. Sharing

reedje27 does not sell or share your data with anyone.

5. Deleting your data

Log out in reedje27 to delete the stored tokens from your computer. You can also remove reedje27's access in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security, Apps and services (Manage app permissions).

6. Children

reedje27 is not meant for children under 13.

7. Contact

Questions about privacy? Send a message to the Pixel Universe channel on YouTube: youtube.com/@reedjerobot27.
